Syncthing is starting

Syncthing 2 stores its index in a new database format and converts the old one the first time it runs. On a large library this takes a while — roughly a minute for every 50,000 files.

Your files are not touched by the conversion and nothing is lost. This page reloads itself every 15 seconds and Syncthing appears as soon as the conversion finishes.

Please do not restart the app while this page is shown. Restarting begins the conversion again from the start and makes the wait longer. Progress is logged to journalctl -u snap.syncthing.syncthing.
